# Matinee privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Matinee is a home screen (launcher) for Google TV and Android TV. It has no accounts, ads or analytics.

## What Matinee reads on your TV

- **Installed apps**, to show them on the home screen.
- **HDMI inputs** and the names connected devices report over HDMI-CEC, to label the inputs row.
- **Media playback info** (title, subtitle, cover image, position) that apps publish while playing, to show what you last played. This needs notification access, which you grant in your TV's settings.
- **Play Store notifications** about available updates, to show them behind the bell. Notifications from every other app are ignored and never read.
- **Network state and memory use**, to show the network icon and RAM meter. When you press "Free up", or turn on automatic freeing, Matinee closes apps from your app list that are waiting in the background. TV system services are never closed.
- **Wi-Fi network name**, only if you turn on "Show Wi-Fi network name". Android shares it only with apps that hold location permission, so Matinee asks for that permission then. Matinee never reads your location; the name is shown on screen and not stored or sent.

## Home button (accessibility permission)

On some TVs the built-in launcher keeps the Home button. If you turn on "Home button opens Matinee", Matinee uses an Android accessibility service for one thing: noticing when the remote's Home button is pressed, so it can open Matinee instead. It does not read screen content, text you type or any other key, and it sends nothing anywhere. Matinee explains this and asks for your agreement before opening the accessibility settings, and you can turn it off there at any time.

All of this stays on your TV, in storage only Matinee can access. Matinee does not send it anywhere. Clearing "last played" in settings or uninstalling Matinee deletes it.

## What leaves your TV

- **Weather.** Matinee sends the latitude and longitude of the city you chose, and the text you type when searching for a city, to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) to fetch the forecast. It never uses or sends your device location. Open-Meteo's own terms apply to those requests.

- **Speed test.** When you move to the network icon, Matinee downloads test data from Cloudflare's public speed test (speed.cloudflare.com) for about three seconds to measure your download speed. Nothing is uploaded, and the result stays on the TV. [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) applies to that request.

Nothing else is sent. Matinee has no servers of its own.

## Children

Matinee is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
